# How to hold a trade (GhostBoard slide)

Source: the "How to Hold" slide from GhostBoard, Advanced Options Data Platform. Saved by the boss; the words are
GhostBoard's, not thestrat.ai's.

## 01 — EMA system 9/14

Use the 9/14 EMA's as an indicator to identify the trend once in a trade to hold the position.

## 02 — Trailing key levels

Trail key levels. If the stock never comes back to the triggering price, there's no reason to be fully out the
position.

## 03 — Strat magnitude

Use the magnitude of the Strat levels and a consistent system so you are able to hold your trades.

---

Note for Trader: the trigger is the level the invalidation rule watches. A 5-minute close back through the trigger
(back inside the setup bar) exits the trade at market. The 9/14 EMA trend hold is reading material; it is not yet a
coded exit.
